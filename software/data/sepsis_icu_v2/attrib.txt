Modifications made for this repository (CC BY 4.0 requires noting changes)
    - Small subset: 400 training and 100 test patients. `source_file` in each
      record gives the original PhysioNet patient file (e.g. p013937).
    - Each record contains a free-text `clinical_summary` per patient; the
      original hourly vitals and laboratory measurements are not included.
    - Sepsis cases were upsampled to roughly 25% of records (vs. ~7% in the
      source data) so the task has a meaningful positive class.
    - The cost model in eval_config.json (treating a patient costs 8x more than
      discharging) is our construction, not part of the original dataset.

This is de-identified data. It is provided for research use only and must not
be used for clinical decision-making.

Used in: Shen et al. (2026), "AI Organizations are More Effective but Less
Aligned than Individual Agents", arXiv:2604.10290 — ICU Treatment Policy task.
